# Class: Tactician

**Tier**: Late game
**Unlock**: Promotes from a lower tier caster class
**Roles**: Support, Damage
**Weapons**: Tomes, Blades
**Class Appeal**: Help allies and hinder foes by spreading status effects across the battlefield.
**Aesthetic**: A bookish battlefield tactician, mage, and medic.
**Central Mechanic**: Tactician revolves around Elemental Alignment, a status effect that is always in one of three states: Raw, Life, or Time. Most tactician skills either have different effects depending on current Alignment or cause the current Alignment to shift.
**Class Resources**
- Manaburst: a resource that recovers charges over time at a rate of 1 every 15 seconds with 3 total charges. Spend on spells that change Alignment.
- Flux: A resource that increases by 1 every time a Manaburst spell is used. 5 charges can be used to execute the special

## Core Skills
**Primary**: Spike
- Deals damage to foes and supports allies either in melee or at range depending on equipped weapon. Against enemies it deals damage normally with a type that depends on elemental alignment. The effect when used on an ally also depends on alignment but varies more:
- Raw: A shield effect worth a set amount of HP.
- Life: A standard healing effect.
- Time: A healing effect that increases if the ally recently took damage.
**Secondary**: Infusion
- Applies a status effect that depends on both target and elemental alignment.
- Raw/Ally (Protect):
- Life/Ally (Regen):
- Time/Ally (Haste):
- Raw/Enemy (Deprotect):
- Life/Enemy (Poison):
- Time/Enemy (Slow):
**Tech 1**: Unmake
**Tech 2**: Restore
**Tech 3**: Accelerate
**Tech 4**: Contagion
- All targets in a small area at range spread their current status effects to other nearby targets. Status effects with a longer duration take priority. Negative status effects are only spread from enemy to enemy and positive status effects are only spread from ally to ally.
**Special**: Checkmate
- Large area attack at range. Damage scales per target based on the number of negative status effects that target is afflicted with. Damage type is split between the current elemental alignment and the element of each status effect.
**Defensive**: Vaccine
- Clear a negative status effect and heal some damage from self and nearby allies. For a short  time after, reduce incoming damage of whatever damage type most recently harmed the target ally and reduce incoming status effect chance for that ally.
**Dash**: Skip
- Instantly teleport a short distance in current facing direction. Immediate but very short invulnerability. Reduces enemy aggro. Cost reduced when aligned to Time.
**Jump**: Tactician's Jump
- Properties change depending on elemental alignment.
- Raw: Double jump off of a shimmering barrier.
- Life: Bestial high jump with small damage effect on landing.
- Time: Slow fall with long air time.
**Crouch**: Standard Crouch

## Additional Skills
Additional skills are not included in a class's rotation and generally have longer cast times, higher mana costs, and no default control binding because you can have a lot of them. On keyboard you can place them on a secondary hotbar and on controller there is a context menu for them.
**Resuscitation**: Revive an ally at low health.
**Tailwind**: Slightly increase an ally's movement speed for an hour.
**Stable Portal:** Cast this spell at two different locations to create a portal between the locations that lasts for half an hour.
