Prop.glb goes here: ShapeLand's one hand-made prop, to keep placing a real model (not a primitive) in use.

What it needs:
- Exported from Blender as glTF binary (.glb) to this folder, as Prop.glb; its source .blend goes in shapeland/art/.
- Sized in meters, standing on its origin (the pivot it's placed by), facing +Z.
- Optional collision: objects named with a -col suffix (Prop-col) are its collision mesh, hidden in the game;
  without one, its whole mesh collides. A simple collision mesh is cheaper.
- Low-poly, flat colors (it's drawn in its placement's color unless the prop gives one).

Then add shapeland/content/props/prop.toml with id = "prop.prop" and model = "Models/Props/Prop.glb", rebuild
the content (Comet › Content › Build) and place it from the place tool's Props section. Delete this note once
the model is in.
