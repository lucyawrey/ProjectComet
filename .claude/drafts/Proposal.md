# Project Anima and Comet: Proposal

*Agent draft of the project lead's front page. The pitch, pillar wording and "what makes it different" are drafts for the project lead to rewrite; the setting is theirs alone.*

## The pitch

Project Anima is an action MMO you play in your browser: one seamless, storybook world with no loading screens, where every item is real, one character can become any class, and half the game is made by its players.

Click a link and you're in the world, the way browser RuneScape once felt, but with approachable action combat that rewards mastery in raids. Gather, craft, trade, decorate a home, join several guilds, roleplay, or chase hard bosses; the game is built so all of those matter.

## Pillars

- **One world, anywhere.** Instant play in a desktop browser, in one seamless world. Walk from town to the mountains without a loading screen.
- **A real, player-shaped world.** Every item is a physical thing that can be traded, dropped, lent or kept forever. The economy, crafting, housing, guilds and player events carry half the game.
- **Be anything.** Every class and craft on one character, carried as Class Crystals, with gentle pressure to specialise and nothing ever lost.
- **Approachable action.** Forgiving action combat, part action RPG and part tab-target MMO: easy to pick up, with boss mechanics deep enough for dedicated raiders.
- **Low-fi charm.** Cute but cool characters and a storybook world, at PS1-to-GameCube fidelity, all made by people.

Underneath the pillars sits one commitment: **anyone can host their own server.**

## What makes it different

- **A seamless action MMO in the browser.** Few MMOs combine browser play, a world without loading screens and action combat with real hitboxes.
- **Physical items.** Items exist in one place at a time. Soulbound items can still be carried and handed back; nothing is sold for real money except a tradeable membership item.
- **Class Crystals.** Switching class is switching a crystal that carries its own gear, skills and look, and every class's progress is kept forever.
- **Fair monetisation.** Free to play with membership; expansions included; no item shop, no loot boxes.
- **Self-hostable.** The foundation is open source, and private servers can run the game's free parts.

## Two layers: Comet and Project Anima

The project has two layers:

- **Comet** is the foundation: servers, netcode, the seamless world, combat rules, persistence, content pipeline and operations tools. It's MIT-licensed and could carry a different, similar game.
- **Project Anima** is the game built on it: its classes, economy, world, story and art.

The split exists so the hardest work stays useful whatever game the team decides to make. Comet is built and proven first, with a tiny demo game of coloured shapes called **ShapeLand**; Project Anima's own systems come once the foundation works.
