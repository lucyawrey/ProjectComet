# Crafting and Housing

## Crafts

The crafts are Crafting, Building, Gathering and Fishing. Any character can level every craft, all to the same cap; there's no choosing one profession. What makes a character a better crafter is their **World Runes**, which come from their class, gear and outfit (see [Classes and Skills](Classes%20and%20Skills.md)). Some classes have craft Runes built in, like an alchemist class with alchemy Runes.

## Crafting is hands-on

Crafting is involved and physical, and each attempt produces a quality score. Better stats make a high score easier, even with weaker play, but skill matters. Reaching a quality threshold for an item permanently unlocks fast, automatic crafting of it. Endgame gear can't reach that threshold on stats alone; players who find clever ways to get there early are welcome to.

Crafters can sign what they make, if they choose.

## Co-crafting

A player who has the materials but not the skill can still get an item made:

- **With another player:** both names go on the signature, and the crafting experience is shared.
- **With an NPC:** for a coin fee, at a fixed, middling quality, and never for top-tier items. Skilled players remain the way to the best gear.

## Gathering and fishing

Gathering and fishing nodes can be per-player (everyone gets their own) or shared (first come, first served), chosen per node by designers. Switching channels to find fresh shared nodes is limited by the channel-move cooldown.

## Housing and guild halls

Houses and guild halls are instanced spaces, reached through doors, gateways or even vehicles in the seamless world, with no loading screen. Building, as a craft, means building houses and guild structures, not building in the open world. Furniture is made of real items, placed in the house.

Houses and guild halls double as community spaces: venues for player events, roleplay and guild life (see [Community](Community.md)). Housing plots and upkeep are among the economy's money sinks.

## Temporary structures

Tents and campfires can be set up in the open world, but they never persist across a server restart: a campfire is used up, and a tent goes back to being an item.
