# Community

Half of Anima is community-driven play, so social systems are a core feature, not an add-on.

## Social systems

- **Guilds:** a character can join several, choosing one as their primary guild. Guilds belong to the region.
- **Chat:** robust chat for parties, guilds, nearby players and private messages.
- **Emotes:** lots of them.
- **Friends** are mutual, and affect which channel you're placed in.
- **Parties** group players for content and keep them on the same channel.
- **Constellations** help strangers keep meeting the same faces (see [World and Travel](World%20and%20Travel.md)).

## Roleplay

Roleplay is encouraged. Characters can have a short **RP profile** (a bio, an RP status such as "in character" or "looking for RP", and a few fields) that others see when inspecting them. Houses and guild halls work as community spaces.

## Events and minigames

An in-game **event board** lists player and guild events, with houses and guild halls as venues. Hosts get no special powers. A few **minigames** come at launch: instanced activities such as team games, fishing contests and races, and casual side games such as card and arcade games.

## Age rating and safety

- **Rating:** aiming for teen (PEGI 12 / ESRB T): fantasy violence, mild language, player interaction, with room for darker moments. No loot boxes or paid randomness.
- **Minimum age:** 13. Some EU countries set a higher age for digital consent; this needs legal review before launch.
- **Player text:** public chat, names, RP profiles and shared spaces stay within the rating. Private chats aren't monitored, but can be reported and follow the same rules of conduct.
- **Defaults:** the profanity filter is on, and adults can turn it off. Players aged 13–17 also default to private messages from friends only.

Moderation, reporting and the 24-hour chat buffer are covered in [Operations](../Comet/Operations.md).
