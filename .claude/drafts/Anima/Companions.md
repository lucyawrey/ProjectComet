# Companions

Companions are creatures that follow you, fight with you and carry you: closer to a light creature-collecting game than to traditional MMO mounts.

## Individuals

Every companion is an individual with its own name, level and appearance. Having "more than one" of a kind means several separate creatures. Companions are their own system, separate from items, but follow item rules wherever they can: they can be traded (by species, with the same Market, Direct and Untradeable settings as items) and some are soulbound.

## Where they are

A companion is always in one of three places:

- **Active:** out in the world with you.
- **Carried:** with you but not summoned, in one of a small, fixed number of companion slots (around four, the same for everyone).
- **Stabled:** kept at a stable in a settlement, house or guild hall.

You can have **one active companion plus a mount**. A mount that also fights takes the active companion's place.

## Tasks

Each companion can do one or more tasks:

- **For anyone:** ground mount, flying mount, follower.
- **For matching classes** (such as a beast master or summoner): fighter, healer, caster. A fighting mount counts as a combat task.

Big companions can carry 2–4 passengers; the owner always steers. Flying needs both a flying mount and the region's Flight Attunement.

## Getting and keeping them

Companions come from capturing wild creatures and from quests, dungeons and vendors. Capturing varies by species: some need capture items, some taming, some a quest. Eggs and capture items are ordinary items until used.

Companions earn experience while active and level up to their species' cap, becoming better at their tasks (a faster, hardier mount; a stronger fighter). Players can name them and dress them in cosmetic gear (saddles, barding, accessories) and learned dyes, but never stat gear.

Releasing a companion sets it free; it's gone. A released soulbound companion can be reclaimed at a recall station, like any soulbound item.
