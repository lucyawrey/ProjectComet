Network stats overlay

A development overlay for tuning under simulated network conditions. F3 shows or hides it, and the choice is remembered. Three layouts with the same four numbers; pick one or mix them.

Example numbers

What the numbers mean

  • Ping: the latest round trip to the server, and the lowest of the last ten (the one the clock trusts).
  • Delay: how far behind the server's moves other players are drawn, and the delay it's growing or shrinking towards. Under a worse network it should settle higher.
  • Holds: the share of other players' moves where their next update came too late, so they stood still briefly and then jumped. This is the number tuning tries to keep low without a large delay.
  • Snap-backs: how often the server corrected your own position. Without the speed cheat (hold C) this should stay at 0; any snap-back means a validation margin is too tight for the network.
  • Shown in every build, not just development builds, so it also works in the web build and, later, the public test build. Off until F3 is pressed.
