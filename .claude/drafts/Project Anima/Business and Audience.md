# Business and Audience

## Who it's for

- **Cosy and social MMO players**, drawn by crafting, housing, roleplay and community.
- **MMO nostalgics**, from browser RuneScape to classic WoW, drawn by instant browser play and a real, player-shaped world.
- **High-end raiders**, drawn by combat that's simple to learn and hard to master, with complex boss mechanics.

## Platforms

Desktop browsers are the main platform: click a link and play, in the spirit of RuneScape's browser days. A desktop app is also planned. Mobile browsers and consoles are not targeted.

## Business model

Project Anima is **free to play with a membership**.

- **The game comes in parts.** The first release has two (early and late game), and each expansion adds another. Free players always have access to an earlier part than members, and get each part eventually as new ones arrive. That means a lower level cap and some later classes locked, but **no feature is barred from free players**.
- **Expansions are included in membership.** Nothing is sold separately.
- **Free and member players play together** freely in the free parts. Later parts sit behind an in-world barrier with a clear message.
- **Items aren't restricted by part.** Free players can buy, trade and hold anything; only level and class requirements limit what they can use.
- **Lapsed members keep everything.** Their level is capped and later classes locked until they renew; nothing is lost.
- **New free accounts** have limited trading, market and public chat until they've played a while or held membership, which blocks throwaway bot accounts without walling off honest players.

### What money buys

The only thing sold for real money is a **tradeable membership item**, like Old School RuneScape's bonds: buy it with money, use it for membership, or sell it to other players for in-game currency. It's sometimes disliked, but it's far better than a cash shop or loot boxes, and one of the better ways to fund a game like this.

- **No item shop.** Nothing else bought with money becomes an item, which protects the economy and the idea that every item is real.
- **No loot boxes** or any other paid randomness.
- **Anima is never sold.**

## Running costs

The proposal covers hosting costs only: game servers (the largest share), Region server processes, PostgreSQL with backups, Valkey for larger regions, and bandwidth for downloads, since every new browser player downloads the client and content. Numbers follow the first load test.

## Comparable games

- **Old School RuneScape:** browser roots, membership and bonds, a player-driven economy.
- **FFXIV:** boss and raid design, outfits, class variety.
- **Classic WoW:** a seamless world and the social MMO feel.
- **Rabbit and Steel:** readable, demanding boss mechanics.

Albion Online, Brighter Shores, Mabinogi, TERA and Blue Protocol are candidates still to research.
