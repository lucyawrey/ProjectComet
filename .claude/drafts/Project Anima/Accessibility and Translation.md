# Accessibility and Translation

## Accessibility

Approachability is a pillar, and that includes players with disabilities. The baseline:

- **Controls:** full rebinding on keyboard and controller, hold or toggle options, and layouts that work one-handed.
- **Vision:** colourblind-safe design, with shapes and icons alongside colour on Rune slots, telegraphs and markers; UI scaling and text size; high-contrast telegraphs.
- **Hearing:** subtitles, and visual cues for important sounds such as boss cues and nearby threats.
- **Motion and flashing:** options to reduce camera shake, motion and flashing effects.

Difficulty helps too: any battle that every player must clear can scale down with an easier setting or NPC support (see [Combat](Combat.md)).

## Translation

The game launches in English, with everything in place to add languages:

- In-game text is extracted automatically by the content build into per-language tables (see [Content Pipeline](../Comet/Content%20Pipeline.md)).
- The interface is built for longer text and other scripts from the start.
- Languages are added by demand.
- **Community translations are welcome:** volunteers can translate through a contributor workflow, with official review before shipping. This helps private servers too.
