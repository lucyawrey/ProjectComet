# Core Loop and Endgame

## Two loops

Project Anima has two loops that feed each other:

- **Adventure:** quests, dungeons and bosses, rewarding drops and experience.
- **Life:** gathering, crafting, trading and housing.

Adventurers need crafted gear and consumables; crafters need materials that adventurers bring back. Neither loop is a side activity: roughly half the game is community-driven play (the economy, crafting, trading, housing, guilds and player events) and half is content we build.

Sessions can be short or long. Meaningful progress should fit in 15–30 minutes (a crafting run, some gathering, a quick dungeon), with longer sessions for raids and events.

## Endgame

Players at the level cap have many directions to go:

- **Raids and hard bosses**, with complex mechanics in the spirit of FFXIV and Rabbit and Steel, for players who want mastery.
- **More classes.** Every class can be learned on one character, and promotions and Soul XP unlocks keep progression going sideways.
- **Crafting and economy mastery:** top-quality gear, rare materials, the market.
- **Collection and housing:** every item can be kept, outfits shown off, houses decorated.
- **Social life:** multiple guilds per character, robust chat, lots of emotes, roleplay with simple RP profiles, houses and guild halls as community spaces, and minigames (a few at first: instanced activities like team games, fishing contests and races, and casual side games like card and arcade games).

## Gear progression

New gear tiers arrive with each expansion, but older gear keeps uses: as outfits, for its Runes, or for niche stats. Gear isn't made worthless overnight, which protects the idea that every item is real.

## Keeping content alive

- **Level sync** lets players join older dungeons at the dungeon's level, with bonus experience for filling groups and for helping new players.
- **Nothing is missable forever.** Seasonal events return each year, and older rewards come back in later runs or from a vendor.
- **Daily and weekly bonuses are optional.** Missed ones accumulate or barely matter; nobody has to log in every day to keep up. Anima already paces play.

## Finding everything

An in-game **content guide** lists every piece of unlockable content (dungeons, raids, classes, crafts, minigames, attunements, companion capture, side stories) with where and how to unlock it. Everything is listed from the start, with details shown on demand and story spoilers hidden until reached; the default view is closer to reveal-as-you-go so it isn't overwhelming. Choosing an entry marks its next step on the map and in the quest tracker, and shows what's in the way (a level, a quest, an attunement). Nobody should have to search a wiki to find a dungeon's unlock quest.

## Content delivery

Expansions add new parts of the game (see [Business and Audience](Business%20and%20Audience.md)). Between them, regular patches every few months add story, dungeons, a raid tier and seasonal events.
