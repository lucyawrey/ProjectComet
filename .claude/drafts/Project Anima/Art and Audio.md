# Art and Audio

## Human-made only

All creative content is made by people: models, textures, readable in-game text, music and sound. AI helps with code only.

## Look

- **Low-poly, low-fi charm:** fidelity leans slightly towards the PS1 era and never beyond the GameCube. Only the resolution and polygon budget are borrowed; PS1 quirks like warping textures and jittering vertices are not recreated. No screen-wide pixel filter.
- **Cute but cool:** mostly human proportions, not overly chibi.
- **A storybook world:** a bright, warm baseline, with some zones going moody, eerie or harsh for contrast (marshes, ruins, dungeons). Fog is a light touch, used only where a zone's mood calls for it.
- **References:** Phantasy Star Online, Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles (also for lighting and mood) and Signalis for textures and character design; FFXIV for variety in character sizes, shapes, outfits and ancestries.

## Characters

- **One shared humanoid skeleton** for all player characters, so animations and outfits work across ancestries. Tails, long ears, wings and horns are optional extra bones; outfits account for them.
- **Ancestries can differ a lot** within a two-armed humanoid body. How far proportions vary is decided with the ancestry designs.
- **Body types:** feminine and masculine, and maybe androgynous. Clothing may have variants, but none is restricted by body type, and no feature is gender-locked.
- **Crystals can transform a character's look** on top of their base form (see [Classes and Skills](Classes%20and%20Skills.md)).
- **What you see is what you get:** World Runes come from the visible outfit, so a character's look matches their world abilities.

## Texturing

Leaning towards a mix: painted trim sheets and vertex-colour shading for environments and most outfits, with individually painted textures for faces, hero gear and showpiece outfits. Both crisp and soft texture filtering will be prototyped before choosing.

## Audio

Music and sound design come later; a composer and sound designer join the team when the project becomes real.
