# New Players

A new player should get from a link to playing in moments, learn the game by playing it, and never feel lost.

## From a link

New players can start straight from a link as a **guest**, with no account. Making an account later keeps the character. Guests can't trade or use public chat, and a guest character is deleted after about 30 days without play (they're told this up front). Everyone answers a neutral age prompt at the start (see [Community](Community.md)).

## Character creation

- **Ancestry** shapes appearance and story only, never stats, so no ancestry is a wrong choice for any class.
- **The creator** offers face, hair and body presets for each ancestry, colours, and a handful of sliders such as height and build. No feature is locked to a body type.
- **Where you start** depends on your ancestry: each has its own prologue area, which becomes the character's home.

Appearance can change later. Barbers in town charge coin for minor changes (hair, makeup). Magical changes, unlocked by a quest, cost Anima and cover bigger changes too. Changing ancestry also costs Anima and needs its own quest.

## The prologue

A short prologue teaches the basics in the character's home area:

1. The character begins without a class, learning movement, dodging and basic combat.
2. Partway through, they receive **Freelancer**, the first Class Crystal, and learn how crystals work.
3. At the end, they promote Freelancer into their first real class.

Choosing a class doesn't move the character away from home, though a class's quests may invite them to travel.

## Learning the rest

Each part of the game has a main story, but it rarely gates systems. Most systems (crafting, companions, Runes and so on) sit behind side quests the main story leads you near; players who know where to go can unlock them early. The content guide shows where everything starts (see [Core Loop and Endgame](Core%20Loop%20and%20Endgame.md)).

## Meeting people

New players carry a visible marker, and experienced players earn bonus experience for helping them, for example in level-synced dungeons.
