# Crafts and gathering

**Layer: mixed** (project lead, 2026-10-04). Base (Comet): crafting as inputs and outputs, recipe storage, crafter signatures and similar basics; gathering nodes with per-player and shared modes. Game (Project Anima): the involved, physical crafting with a quality score and the auto-craft threshold, no craft attunement as a policy, and Runes that improve crafts (including locked class Runes).

Agent notes on crafts (Crafting, Building, Gathering, Fishing). Decisions made with the project lead on 2026-10-03. Housing, guild halls and temporary structures are in `housing.md`: crafters interact with them, but they aren't part of crafts (project lead, 2026-10-04). Topic covered; no open questions recorded yet.

## Decided

- **No craft attunement.** Any player can level every craft freely, all to the same level cap. (Replaces both the Craft Crystal and class-linked craft ideas in the notes.)
- **Runes make you better at specific crafts.**
- **Classes can include locked Runes** that are masterable and extractable, like gear Runes. Some are craft World Runes (e.g. an Alchemist class with alchemy Runes built in).
  - Locked class Runes visually occupy a slot (for simplicity), but designers don't count them against the class's slot budget.
  - Mastery builds up on the owner's class entry (character-bound), so borrowers don't build it.
  - Most Runes are extractable; the system also supports non-extractable Runes. Classes supplying their Runes to the market is fine (mastery may take a while).
- **Gathering and fishing nodes** support both per-player and shared modes, chosen per node by designers. Fine if one pattern wins out later based on how players respond.
- **Crafting is involved and physical**, producing a total quality score.
  - Higher stats make a high score easier, even with worse play.
  - Reaching a quality threshold permanently unlocks fast (automatic) crafting for that item.
  - Endgame gear can't reach the auto-craft threshold on stats alone. Players finding clever tricks to unlock auto-craft early is fine.
