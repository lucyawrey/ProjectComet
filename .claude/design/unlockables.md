# Unlockables

Agent notes on unlockables: progression not tied to items, companions, or class and craft levels. Decisions made with the project lead on 2026-10-03. Topic covered.

## Decided

- **Unlockables are learned flags** (see `items.md`), not a separate progression system.
- **"Soul" is theming, not a technical concept.** Soul XP and Anima are player-facing names; under the hood they're ordinary character data.
- **Anima Capacity is a separate value**, not a flag. It's raised by:
  - spending Soul XP,
  - quest rewards,
  - rare items, each usable once per character.
- **Flight Attunements are per region.** Flying needs both a flying mount and the region's attunement (see `companions.md`).
- **Teleportation Attunements are per destination.**
- **Attunements are earned through exploration and quests.**
- **Teleporting to an attuned destination costs Anima.**
- **The XP-to-Soul toggle** (see `classes.md`) is an unlockable.
